Our Heritage & Philosophy

Six Decades of Intellectual Rigor & Coastal Stewardship.

Founded in 1962 along a ridge of old-growth coastal redwoods, Redwood Academy was built on a singular conviction: young minds flourish when deep scholarly discipline meets real-world exploration.

From Harkness-style humanities seminars to field ecology research in our living forest laboratory, every student is mentored individually to think critically, communicate persuasively, and act with integrity.

Our Educational Charter

To unite analytical mastery, creative expression, and environmental responsibility across K–12.

National Distinction

WASC & NAIS accredited independent preparatory academy with National Blue Ribbon honors.

Academic Architecture

A Seamless K–12 Preparatory Journey

Explore our three academic divisions—designed to cultivate foundational wonder in Lower School, analytical independence in Middle School, and collegiate mastery in Upper School.

Canopy Field Research

Watershed hydrology, coastal botany, and climate telemetry stations across our 42-acre redwood preserve.

Performing Arts Conservatory

Chamber symphony, jazz ensemble, black-box theater, ceramics, and digital cinema production.
