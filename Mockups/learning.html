About Cedar Academy

Seven Decades of Character, Scholarship, and Stewardship.

Founded in 1954 amidst forty-two acres of native cedar groves, Cedar Academy believes education should nurture both intellect and integrity. Our classrooms combine seminar-style discussion, modern scientific laboratories, and outdoor environmental learning.

Every student is known by name, mentored individually by dedicated educators, and encouraged to discover their unique voice across academics, athletics, service, and the arts.

Our Mission

To cultivate disciplined inquiry, ethical leadership, and lifelong curiosity in every learner.

Accreditation

Fully accredited K–12 independent preparatory school with National Blue Ribbon distinction.

“Like the cedar tree that thrives through every season, we equip our students with deep intellectual roots and the resilience to reach toward their highest potential.”

EW
